Frequently asked questions

What does this assistant do?

It answers questions about the credit union's rates, fees, products and branches. It reads five saved copies (snapshots) of the credit union website and gives one answer with its sources.

Where do the answers come from?

Only from the five snapshots, dated May 12, 2026 to September 28, 2026. The assistant does not read the live website and does not use other sources.

What does "Current" mean?

"Current" means the newest copy of the website, September 28, 2026. Today's date can be later. The assistant cannot know about changes after the newest copy.

How do I ask about a past date?

Open the As of menu under the message box. Select a snapshot date, or select Another date… and pick the month, day and year. You can also write the month in your question, for example "as of May 2026". The assistant uses the newest copy on or before that date. It never uses a later copy for an earlier date. For a date before May 12, 2026, it says that it does not know.

Can I ask a follow-up question?

Yes. After "What's the APR for a 60-month auto loan on a 2025 vehicle?", you can ask "What about 72 months?". The assistant joins the follow-up to your last question and shows the joined question as Read as. Select New chat to start again without the last question.

Where do I see the sources?

Each answer has a Sources box under it. Open it to see the pages, the date of each copy and the quoted text.

Two pages show different values. Which value does it use?

It does not take a majority vote. The official rate page wins over product pages, and product pages win over promotions, news, blogs and calculators. A newer effective date wins over an older one. An offer that has ended loses. In the Sources box, Why this value gives the reason and Other values on the website lists the values that were not chosen.

Why does it sometimes say "I don't know"?

It says this when the snapshots do not state the fact, or when the evidence does not match your question closely. For example, the website shows no rate for a 7/1 adjustable-rate mortgage. The assistant does not guess, and an "I don't know" answer contains no numbers.

What is the difference between APR, APY and dividend rate?

APR (annual percentage rate) is the yearly cost of a loan. APY (annual percentage yield) is the yearly return on savings, including compounding. The dividend rate is the rate paid on savings before compounding. The assistant keeps these three separate.

Why does an answer show several rows?

Many products have tiers by balance, term, loan amount or vehicle year. If you do not name a tier, the assistant shows the whole table. Name the tier, for example "$15,000 balance" or "84 months on a 2024 vehicle", to get one row.

Can I ask how a value changed?

Yes. Ask, for example, "Did the 10-month jumbo certificate rate change?" The assistant answers for each snapshot and shows the earlier and the current value.

What does "a page that was last seen on" mean?

The fact comes from a page that was later removed from the website. The assistant uses such a page only when no current page answers, and it tells you that the value may be out of date.

Do the source links show the same text?

Not always. A link opens the live website, which can be different from the saved copy. The quote under each source shows the text from the saved copy.

Is my conversation stored or sent anywhere?

No. The app runs on your computer and does not send data out. The conversation stays in this browser tab only. It is deleted when you reload the page or select New chat.

Can I use an answer to make a financial decision?

Use it for information only. It is not financial advice. Rates and fees change, so confirm with the credit union before you act.

How fast is it?

The first start reads all pages and takes about one minute. The app then saves what it learned, so later starts take a few seconds. Most answers take less than 0.1 seconds.

Are there keyboard shortcuts?

Press / to go to the message box. Press Enter to send, and Shift + Enter for a new line. Use the arrow keys to move between tabs.
